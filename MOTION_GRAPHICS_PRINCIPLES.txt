1. THE FUNDAMENTAL MISTAKE: THE "SLIDE SHOW" TRAP
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- WHAT AMATEUR / TEMPLATE GENERATORS DO:
  * Beat 1 (0s-3s): Show Card A with a title and 2 circular icons.
  * 3s mark: Screen wipes or clears completely.
  * Beat 2 (3s-6s): Show Card B with another title and 2 circular icons.
  * Result: The viewer feels like they are watching a boring automated PowerPoint carousel.
    It feels disjointed, static, robotic, and cheap.

- WHAT TOP-TIER MOTION GRAPHICS (ALIGHT MOTION / APPLE / CLAUDE / TECH REELS) DO:
  * There are NO isolated slides. The entire video is ONE CONTINUOUS LIVING CANVAS.
  * The camera MOVES THROUGH space: zooming into a detail, panning across a connected network,
    whip-scaling to a hero metric, or zooming out into an expansive constellation.
  * Elements PERSIST and EVOLVE: Objects introduced in Beat 1 don't just disappear; they transform,
    anchor to the side, spawn child connections, or return at the finale.

4. THE 7 MANDATORY LAWS FOR THE AGENT TO CREATE PROFESSIONAL MOTION:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

LAW 1: THE SINGLE-CANVAS / CONTINUOUS WORLD LAW
  Never clear the whole screen to black or white between beats.
  Maintain a unified visual world (grid, ambient nebula, particles, or depth plane).
  Transitions must be:
  - Camera Pan/Zoom: Move the virtual camera to a new sector of the canvas.
  - Morphing / Expansion: An existing element unfolds or splits into the next diagram.
  - Progressive Additions: Keep the current diagram on screen and build the next layer upon it.

LAW 2: BAN REPETITIVE CIRCLES & PLACEHOLDER ORBS
  Never draw generic circular badges for everything.
  Every visual must be a LITERAL or HIGH-TECH METAPHOR of the exact spoken topic:
  - Technical / Code: Terminal windows ($ git commit), branching commit graphs (DAGs),
    syntax diff cards with green + and red - lines, bytecode stacks, memory registers.
  - Systems / Architecture: Cloud clusters, server racks, laser data pipelines, packet queues.
  - Metrics / Performance: Horizontal latency meters, speedometers, live telemetry dials.
  - Social / Product: Glassmorphic UI cards, profile pills, star ratings, toggle switches.

LAW 3: SCALE DIVERSITY (MACRO VS MICRO)
  A professional composition mixes extreme scales:
  - Macro Hero: One huge element that dominates 40-60% of the screen (giant clock hand, massive terminal).
  - Micro Details: Small, crisp data tags, coordinate stamps (x, y, z), git commit hashes (#a1b2c),
    status indicators (● ACTIVE, 200 OK).
  - Atmospheric Layer: Faint background watermarks, subtle grid meshes, ambient floating particles.

LAW 4: PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE (WATCHING IT BUILD)
  Never drop a finished diagram onto the screen all at once.
  Let the viewer see it happen in sync with the narration:
  - Frame 0: Root node drops with a punchy bounce.
  - Frame 12: Connecting bezier line draws itself (strokeDashoffset from 100% to 0%).
  - Frame 24: Target node blooms outward with elastic overshoot.
  - Frame 36: Data packet travels along the line.

LAW 5: SEAMLESS CAMERA CHOREOGRAPHY
  The camera must never stand still:
  - Drift: Slow, steady 2-3% zoom or parallax drift while text is spoken.
  - Kinetic Moves: Fast whip-pan or snap-zoom when switching focus to the next beat.
  - Return: At the final beat or outro, zoom out or pan back to the hero anchor to close the story.

LAW 6: LAYERED Z-DEPTH COMPOSITION
  Every frame must have at least 4 distinct depth layers:
  - Layer 1 (Z: 0): Ambient Background & moving grid / drift particles.
  - Layer 2 (Z: 10): Secondary context, faint watermarks, connection tracks.
  - Layer 3 (Z: 20): Primary Hero Subject (terminal, branch tree, UI cards).
  - Layer 4 (Z: 30): Kinetic typography and active karaoke captions locked to the voice.

LAW 7: BRANDED CONTINUOUS OUTRO
  The transition to the final Bittrix Technologies outro must be seamless:
  - The hero objects of the video resolve or fly inward.
  - The hexagonal gear, rolling track, and glowing purple underline materialize organically.
  - Narration flows without any audio gap or silence straight into the closing brand beat.
